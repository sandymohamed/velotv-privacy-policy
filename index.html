VeloTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

VeloTV is an IPTV player application that allows users to play media content provided through external playlist servers. This policy explains how information may be processed when using the application.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the application version and configuration, the following information may be processed:

  • MAC address and login credentials: Versions with authentication may use a MAC address and password to authenticate with a service provider's API.
  • Playlist and media information: The application connects to external servers to retrieve channels, movies, series, and other playlist content.
  • Technical connection information: External servers may receive IP addresses, connection timestamps, and request details during normal network operations.

The current testing version may use a predefined MAC address and a fixed demo playlist instead of requiring users to log in. Information processing may differ between versions.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to retrieve and play content, connect to playlist servers, authenticate users when enabled, and support the technical operation of network connections.

4. Third-Party Services

VeloTV connects to external playlist and media servers. These services may process connection information or other data needed to provide their services. Their data practices are governed by their own policies.

VeloTV currently does not integrate AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Sentry, or Crashlytics.

5. Data Sharing

Information may be transmitted to the configured playlist server or service provider when necessary for content retrieval or authentication. External providers may independently process or retain information according to their own practices.

6. Data Retention and Security

Data retention by external playlist or authentication providers depends on their respective practices. VeloTV cannot control the retention policies of independently operated servers. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

VeloTV is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you have concerns about information processed in connection with the application, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at the same URL.
